ABOUT FORMA ARCHITECTS & BUILDERS

ARCHITECTURAL VISION.
ENGINEERED CERTAINTY.

FORMA is an award-winning integrated architectural studio and turnkey construction firm. For over 16 years, we have merged sculptural architectural elegance with precision structural engineering, delivering single-source turnkey luxury residences, urban homes, and civic landmarks across South India and internationally.

01 / DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

MONOLITHIC PURITY & CLIMATIC HARMONY

We believe architecture is an enduring sensory experience. Our projects reject superficial decoration in favor of authentic raw materials: fair-faced monolithic concrete, structural steel, locally quarried granite, and thermal low-iron glass. Each space is carefully sculpted to capture prevailing wind currents, choreograph natural daylight, and foster deep tranquility.

By treating light as a primary building material, we create buildings that transform throughout the day, providing an ever-changing canvas of shadows, textures, and spatial warmth.

02 / TURNKEY SYNERGY

UNIFIED DESIGN-BUILD EXECUTION

Traditional construction is fractured between architects who don't construct and contractors who don't understand architectural vision. This disjoint causes budget escalations, design dilution, and timeline delays.

FORMA eliminates this gap. Our single-entity turnkey model integrates architectural design, structural engineering, statutory approvals, procurement, civil execution, MEP engineering, and interior bespoke joinery under a single accountable contract with guaranteed timelines and transparent pricing.
